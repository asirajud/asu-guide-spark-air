Sol.

An AI campus compass — built entirely on ASU's AI Research Platform.

qwen35-27b qwen3-vl-32b-instruct qwen3-embedding-4b 48 open-weight AIR models, one gateway

The problem

ASU generates more campus information than any student can read, let alone act on.

  • 01 1,962 upcoming events on Sun Devil CentralOne public iCal feed, zero personal search.
  • 02 Which walk has shade at 3 PM in 105°FThe forecast and the tree cover both exist. Nothing answers the question.
  • 03 Course material scattered across photos and PDFsWhiteboard shots, lecture slides — never searchable, never connected.

The idea

Sol turns any firehose of unstructured ASU information into something a student can search, understand, and act on — powered entirely by ASU's AI Research Platform.

ingest→ embed→ hybrid search→ tool-call to act
Corpus 1 — campus events Corpus 2 — your notebooks

Demo 1 — events

Snap a flyer. Sol finds the event, books the seat — and recovers when it guesses wrong.

Vision reads the flyer, hybrid BM25 + embedding search matches it against the live feed, and one tool-calling loop handles the failure case live, on camera.

reserve me a spot for the first one
›reserve_spot — guessed id, fails
›model reads the JSON-Schema error
›search_events — recovers the real id
›reserve_spot — succeeds
3 tool calls, 1 turn, live.

Demo 2 — HeatRoute

An AI doesn't need a page. It reaches for a tool.

Ask a normal question — Sol decides on its own that the answer needs a walking-route tool, calls it, and folds the plan back into the conversation.

MU to Hayden at 12:30 without frying
›plan_heat_route — 22ms tool call
›shade-aware path, honest time estimate
5.6s end to end, no map click required.

Demo 3 — notebooks

Same engine, second corpus — this time it's your own notes.

Upload page photos in order. Sol OCRs each one, keeps a running digest as it goes, then answers questions from the digest plus the raw pages — citing the page it came from. Ships behind an admin feature switch, off by default.
